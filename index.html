Instructions

Before we begin, here are some instructions.

1

Get Together in a Team

Get together with your friends to make teams of 4.

2

Sit Down and Raise Your Hands

After you find your team, sit down quietly and raise your hand. We will hand you a whiteboard.

Rules

Before we begin, here's how Chess Jeopardy works.

1

Choose a Question

Select any available category and point value from the Jeopardy board.

2

Answer the Question

Your team gets a chance to answer the chess question shown within 60 seconds. All teams that get the question correct will earn the points.

3

Earn Points

Correct answers earn the points shown on the board.

4

Have Fun!

Work together, test your chess knowledge, and enjoy the game.

♟️ Remember: Once a question has been selected, it cannot be selected again.
